# Stock Expert AI

Stock Expert AI is a free US-stock research platform for self-directed investors. Explore company information, stock screeners and dated AI-written analysis on the [hosted website](https://www.stockexpertai.com/).

This repository is a public project overview. It does not currently include the application's source code.

Public research pages can be read without an account. A free account adds watchlists, AI chat, portfolio tools and paper trading. AI tools have usage limits; see the [current pricing and allowances](https://www.stockexpertai.com/pricing).

MoonshotScore V2 summarizes five research pillars: business quality, financial safety, valuation, growth durability and momentum. A current score is shown only for eligible companies where a usable V2 result is available. Funds and ETFs do not receive a MoonshotScore.

A quote refresh, score calculation and AI analysis have separate dates. Read each analysis as of its writing date. Scores are analytical signals, not predictions of returns. Stock Expert AI provides educational research and is not a broker or registered investment adviser.
